## Official requirements (summary)
- The program makes up to 50,000 immigrant visas available each year, selected at random from all entries, for people from countries with low rates of immigration to the U.S. [[fonte:uscis-dv]].
- It is run by the **Department of State**. Most selectees live abroad and go through consular processing [[fonte:uscis-dv]].
- Eligible countries and each year's rules are published in the Department of State's official instructions: **check whether your country can take part in the year you plan to enter**.
- The visa must be completed by September 30 of the fiscal year of the lottery; unused visas do not carry over [[fonte:uscis-dv]].
- Watch out for scams: the Department of State keeps a fraud warning page [[fonte:uscis-dv]].

## What you can do now
- Read the year's official instructions on the Department of State website (travel.state.gov).
- Have a plan that does not depend only on the lottery.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
