## Official requirements (summary)
- Be a national of a **treaty country**. The current list is kept by the Department of State on the "Treaty Countries" page at travel.state.gov [[fonte:uscis-e2]].
- Have invested (or be actively investing) a substantial amount in a real, operating business, with the capital at risk and from lawful sources; the business cannot be marginal [[fonte:uscis-e2]].
- Own at least 50% of the business or have operational control, and come solely to develop and direct it [[fonte:uscis-e2]].
- Initial stay of up to 2 years, with extensions of up to 2 years and no limit on their number; you must intend to leave when the status ends [[fonte:uscis-e2]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may come as dependents, regardless of nationality. Spouses in E-2 or E-2S status are generally authorized to work [[fonte:uscis-e2]].

## Official links to check
U.S. government pages that USCIS itself points to. Open them in your browser and check the current rules:
- [Treaty Countries (Department of State)](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/visa-information-resources/fees/treaty.html): the official list of treaty countries for E-1 and E-2 visas. Check whether any of your nationalities is on it.
