## Official requirements (summary)
- Only for nationals of Australia, to work in a specialty occupation (a bachelor's or higher degree in the field, or equivalent) [[fonte:uscis-e3]].
- Requires a job offer and a Labor Condition Application (LCA) [[fonte:uscis-e3]].
- Initial stay of 2 years, with extensions of up to 2 years [[fonte:uscis-e3]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may come as dependents; the spouse is authorized to work and the children may not work [[fonte:uscis-e3]].
