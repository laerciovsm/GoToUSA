## Official requirements (summary)
- Sustained national or international acclaim in the field [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- Meet at least 3 of 10 evidence criteria (such as awards, memberships, published material about the person, judging others' work, original contributions, articles, exhibitions, leading roles, high salary, commercial success in the performing arts) **or** show a one-time major internationally recognized achievement [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- No job offer or labor certification required. The person may file on their own, using Form I-140 [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].

## Family
Once the I-140 is approved, the spouse and unmarried children under 21 may apply for admission (E-14 and E-15 status) [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].

## What you can do now
- Gather evidence for each achievement (awards, publications, press, letters).
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
