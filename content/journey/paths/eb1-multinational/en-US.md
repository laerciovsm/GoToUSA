title: EB-1 Green Card: multinational manager or executive
summary: Permanent residence for managers and executives transferred by a company that operates inside and outside the U.S.
reasons:
  multinational: Works at a multinational with a U.S. branch for more than 1 year.
  level: Manager, executive, or owner role reported.
---

## Official requirements (summary)
- Worked outside the U.S. for at least 1 year in the 3 years before the petition (or before the most recent lawful admission, if already working for the U.S. employer) [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- The U.S. employer must have done business for at least 1 year and have a qualifying relationship with the foreign employer; the job must be managerial or executive [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- The employer files Form I-140. No labor certification required [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may come along, under the rules on the official page [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].

## What you can do now
- Talk to your company's HR about transfer programs.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
