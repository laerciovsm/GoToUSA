## Official requirements (summary)
- International recognition in an academic field and at least 3 years of teaching or research experience [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- Coming for a tenure or tenure-track teaching position, or a comparable research position, at a university, higher-education institution, or private employer [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- Meet at least 2 of 6 criteria (major prizes, memberships, published material by others about your work, judging others' work, original research contributions, authorship of scholarly works) [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].
- A job offer is required; the employer files Form I-140. No labor certification required [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may come along, under the rules on the official page [[fonte:uscis-eb1]].

## What you can do now
- Organize your list of publications, citations, and peer-review work.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
