  degree: Master's or doctorate reported.

## Official requirements (summary)
- First, be eligible for EB-2 (advanced degree or exceptional ability) [[fonte:uscis-eb2]].
- USCIS weighs three prongs: the endeavor has substantial merit and national importance; the person is well positioned to advance it; and, on balance, it would benefit the U.S. to waive the job offer and labor certification [[fonte:uscis-eb2]].
- The law does not define which endeavors qualify [[fonte:uscis-eb2]].
- The person may file Form I-140 on their own, without an employer, with Form ETA-9089, Appendix A [[fonte:uscis-eb2]].

## Family
Once the I-140 is approved, the spouse and unmarried children under 21 may apply for admission (E-21 and E-22 status) [[fonte:uscis-eb2]].

## What you can do now
- Write a few lines about your endeavor in the U.S. and why it matters to the country.
- Gather evidence of what you have already done in this area.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
