## Official requirements (summary)
- Invest **$1,050,000**, or **$800,000** in a targeted employment area, in a new commercial enterprise that employs at least 10 full-time U.S. workers [[fonte:uscis-permanent-workers]].
- Plan to create or preserve 10 permanent full-time jobs [[fonte:uscis-eb5]].
- There is a Regional Center Program, with visas set aside for investments tied to USCIS-approved regional centers [[fonte:uscis-eb5]].
- No labor certification required [[fonte:uscis-permanent-workers]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 are eligible alongside the investor [[fonte:uscis-eb5]].

## What you can do now
- Organize proof that your money comes from lawful sources.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
