## Official requirements (summary)
- Immediate relatives are: the spouse of a U.S. citizen, the unmarried child under 21 of a U.S. citizen, and the parent of a U.S. citizen who is at least 21 [[fonte:uscis-immediate-relatives]].
- Immigrant visas for immediate relatives are **unlimited**: they are always available [[fonte:uscis-immediate-relatives]].
- The process uses Form I-130 (Petition for Alien Relative), filed by the relative [[fonte:uscis-immediate-relatives]].
- Sons and daughters 21 or older and married sons and daughters of citizens fall under the family preference categories [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].

## What you can do now
- Talk to your relative in the U.S. about the process.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
