---
title: Family Green Card: preference categories
summary: Permanent residence for other relatives of U.S. citizens and permanent residents, with a limited number of visas per year.
reasons:
  parent: U.S. citizen parent reported (sons and daughters 21 or older, and married sons and daughters).
  sibling: U.S. citizen brother or sister reported.
  spouse-resident: Spouse with a Green Card reported.
  parent-resident: Parent with a Green Card reported.
---

## Official requirements (summary)
- **F1:** unmarried sons and daughters (21 or older) of U.S. citizens [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].
- **F2A:** spouses and children (unmarried and under 21) of permanent residents [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].
- **F2B:** unmarried sons and daughters (21 or older) of permanent residents [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].
- **F3:** married sons and daughters of U.S. citizens [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].
- **F4:** brothers and sisters of U.S. citizens (if the citizen is 21 or older) [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].
- The petition uses Form I-130. Visa availability is tracked in the Visa Bulletin, published by the Department of State [[fonte:uscis-family-preference]].

## What you can do now
- Talk to your relative in the U.S. about the process and follow the Visa Bulletin.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
