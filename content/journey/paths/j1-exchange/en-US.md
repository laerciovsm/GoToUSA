## Official requirements (summary)
- Take part in an approved program sponsored by an entity designated by the Department of State. Examples: professors, researchers, students, trainees, au pairs, camp counselors [[fonte:uscis-exchange-visitors]].
- The first step is getting Form DS-2019 from the sponsor; then you apply for the visa at a consulate. Entry is not allowed more than 30 days before the program starts [[fonte:uscis-exchange-visitors]].
- Work is only allowed under the terms of the program [[fonte:uscis-exchange-visitors]].
- Some participants are subject to a home residence requirement (Form I-612 is used to request a waiver); check the official source [[fonte:uscis-exchange-visitors]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may hold J-2 status and may apply for work authorization (Form I-765), but the J-1's income cannot depend on that work [[fonte:uscis-exchange-visitors]].

## What you can do now
- Look for programs with a sponsor designated by the Department of State.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
