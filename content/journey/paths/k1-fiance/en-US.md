## Official requirements (summary)
- The petitioner must be a U.S. citizen [[fonte:uscis-k1]].
- The couple must intend to marry within 90 days of admission on the K-1, and both must be legally free to marry [[fonte:uscis-k1]].
- The couple must have met in person at least once within the 2 years before filing (waivers exist) [[fonte:uscis-k1]].
- The citizen files Form I-129F [[fonte:uscis-k1]].
- The official page was last updated in 2018: check the current version before deciding.

## Family
Unmarried children under 21 of the fiancé(e) may get a K-2 visa if listed on Form I-129F [[fonte:uscis-k1]].
