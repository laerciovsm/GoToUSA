  multinational: Works at a multinational with a U.S. branch for more than 1 year.

## Official requirements (summary)
- The U.S. employer must have a qualifying relationship (parent, branch, subsidiary, or affiliate) with the foreign company, and both must do business in the U.S. and at least one other country [[fonte:uscis-l1a]].
- Generally, one continuous year working for the company abroad within the 3 years before admission [[fonte:uscis-l1a]] [[fonte:uscis-l1b]].
- **L-1A:** executive or managerial role; maximum stay of 7 years [[fonte:uscis-l1a]].
- **L-1B:** specialized knowledge of the company's products, services, or processes; maximum stay of 5 years [[fonte:uscis-l1b]].
- There are also rules for opening a **new office** in the U.S., with an initial stay of up to 1 year [[fonte:uscis-l1a]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may come in L-2 status. Spouses in L-2S status are employment authorized incident to status [[fonte:uscis-l1a]].

## What you can do now
- Talk to HR about a possible transfer.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
