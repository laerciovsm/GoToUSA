## Official requirements (summary)
- **O-1A** (sciences, education, business, athletics): sustained national or international acclaim, among the small percentage at the very top of the field [[fonte:uscis-o1]].
- **O-1B** (arts, film, and TV): distinction in the arts, or extraordinary achievement in film and TV [[fonte:uscis-o1]].
- The petition includes at least 3 different types of documentation from the regulations and a written advisory opinion from a peer group or expert [[fonte:uscis-o1]].
- A U.S. employer or agent files Form I-129. Initial stay of up to 3 years, with extensions of up to 1 year [[fonte:uscis-o1]].

## Family
The spouse and children under 21 may seek O-3 status: they cannot work but may study [[fonte:uscis-o1]].

## What you can do now
- Gather evidence of your achievements and contacts of experts in your field.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
