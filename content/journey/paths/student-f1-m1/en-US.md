## Official requirements (summary)
- **F-1:** full-time academic students. **M-1:** full-time vocational students [[fonte:uscis-students]].
- **Work is limited.** On F-1, off-campus work is not allowed during the first academic year; after that, there are CPT, OPT, and the STEM OPT extension, always related to the field of study and authorized before starting. On M-1, practical training is only allowed after completing studies [[fonte:uscis-students-employment]].
- Off-campus work must be authorized before it begins [[fonte:uscis-students-employment]].

## Family
The spouse and children may hold F-2 or M-2 status [[fonte:uscis-students]].

## Official links to check
U.S. government pages that USCIS itself points to. Open them in your browser and check the current rules:
- [Student and Exchange Visitor Program, SEVP (ICE)](https://www.ice.gov/sevis): the agency that oversees F and M schools and students.

## What you can do now
- Research schools and their admission requirements, including English.
- Plan how to pay for school and housing without relying on work.
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
