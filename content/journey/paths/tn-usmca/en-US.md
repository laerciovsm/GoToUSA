## Official requirements (summary)
- Be a citizen of Canada or Mexico [[fonte:uscis-tn]].
- The profession must be in the USMCA, the U.S. position must require that professional, and you need a prearranged full-time or part-time job with a U.S. employer [[fonte:uscis-tn]].
- Initial stay of up to 3 years, with extensions [[fonte:uscis-tn]].

## Family
The spouse and unmarried children under 21 may seek TD status: they cannot work but may study [[fonte:uscis-tn]].

## What you can do now
- Take your questions to a licensed **immigration attorney**: they evaluate your case and handle the process.
