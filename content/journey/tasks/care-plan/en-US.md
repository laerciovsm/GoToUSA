---
title: Plan care during the move
why: People who need support feel changes more. Planning ahead makes the transition calmer for everyone.
subtasks:
  routine: Describe the routine and what helps day to day
  caregivers: Agree on who provides care at each stage of the move
  health-info: Gather health and follow-up information
  transition: Think about how to prepare this person for the move
---

Write down in the **Record** the routine, what calms and what helps, who provides care and what needs to continue in the U.S. Official rules (such as services and benefits) appear in their own tasks, always with the source.
