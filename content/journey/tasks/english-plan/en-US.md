title: Create your English plan
why: English opens doors at work, at school and in daily life. A plan with a day and time actually happens.
subtasks:
  choose-goal: Choose a goal for the next few months
  when-where: Decide when and where you'll study
  first-week: Complete the first week
---

Plans with "when and where" work better than intentions. Example: "Tuesdays and Thursdays, 8 p.m., at the living room table". Start small and celebrate every week you complete.
