---
title: Talk about the move as a family
why: Deciding together avoids surprises and gives you strength on hard days.
subtasks:
  pick-moment: Set aside an unhurried moment
  share-why: Share your why
  hear-doubts: Listen to everyone's doubts
  write-agreements: Write down what you agreed on
---

Pick a calm moment, with phones put away. Explain why you want this move and listen carefully to how each person feels. You don't need to decide everything today: what matters is that everyone feels part of the journey.

At the end, use this task's **Record** to write down what you agreed on and the next steps.
