---
title: Check the passport
why: The passport is the basis for almost every other step. Knowing each person's situation avoids a rush later.
subtasks:
  find: Find the passport
  check: Check the expiration date
  note: Write down the situation in the Record
documents:
  passport: Passport
---

Check the passport and write down in the **Record** whether you have it, have requested it or still need it, and the expiration date. Don't write down the passport number. Validity and issuance rules appear in their own tasks, with the official source from your country and the U.S.
