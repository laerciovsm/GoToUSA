GoToUSA is a **support for your journey**. Any immigration process should be handled by an immigration attorney or another qualified professional.

Under USCIS rules, you may be represented by: attorneys who are members in good standing of the bar of a U.S. state, territory, or the District of Columbia; representatives accredited by the Department of Justice (EOIR) who work for recognized organizations; and law students or graduates supervised by them [[fonte:uscis-legal-services]].

- **Beware of "notarios":** in the U.S., a notary public is **not** an attorney and cannot represent you in immigration cases [[fonte:uscis-legal-services]].
- **Check the license:** ask for the attorney's current licensing document and confirm it with the state agency; the Department of Justice keeps lists of accredited representatives, disciplined practitioners, and free or low-cost services [[fonte:uscis-legal-services]].
